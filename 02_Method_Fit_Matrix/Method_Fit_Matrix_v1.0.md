PhD in Deep Tech — UNMSM · Session 2 · Edgar Aldair Quispe Huaman

## 1. Refined research question

Given a fixed memory budget on limited hardware, can a budget-aware method for selecting quantization precision, adapter rank and target modules reach a better quality–cost trade-off than default QLoRA/LoRA configurations, and do its design principles hold across model families, Spanish-language tasks and hardware classes?

## 2. Candidate methods (all viable)

- **A. Design Science.** Build the selection method and evaluate it through factorial experiments (rigor cycle) and confirmatory paired comparisons.
- **B. Experimental characterization study.** Map the configuration space with a factorial design and report effects and the Pareto frontier, without building an artifact.
- **C. Simulation / analytical modeling.** Estimate memory, latency and quality from analytical cost models and scaling relations instead of running every configuration.

## 4. Weights: highest and lowest

- **Highest, E (30):** a method that cannot express "knowledge through making" would contradict my Session 1 paradigm, and no venue fit can compensate for that.
- **Lowest, D (10):** data availability is not a risk in my study, because I generate the data myself from open models and public benchmarks.

## 5. Winning method: Design Science

I choose Design Science because my contribution is a **budget-aware configuration-selection artifact with demonstrated utility**, plus transferable design principles. The factorial experiments are its rigor cycle, not the contribution itself. I reject the **experimental characterization study** because it would map the trade-off space without giving practitioners a procedure to act on it. I reject **simulation** because analytical cost models can predict memory, but they cannot establish the quality degradation of 3–4-bit adaptation on Spanish tasks, which is an empirical property.

*(Gregor & Hevner, 2013: the contribution type is **improvement**, a new solution to a known problem. If the selection procedure ends up reusing an existing optimizer such as Bayesian optimization, it should be reclassified as exaptation.)*

## 6. Exit commitment

"I will use Design Science with factorial experimental evaluation because my contribution is a selection artifact whose utility must be demonstrated under real memory budgets, and I reject simulation because, specifically, analytical models cannot measure quality loss from low-bit quantization on Spanish-language tasks."

**Open tension:** feasibility (F = 3) is the weak point of the winning method; generalizing across hardware classes may exceed my compute budget.
