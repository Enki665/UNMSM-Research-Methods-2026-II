| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Developer | Edgar Aldair Quispe Huaman, PhD in Deep Tech, UNMSM |
| Artifact | Diagnosis and planning agents of a digital twin for underground load-haul-dump loaders |
| Diagnosis agent | Mahalanobis distance to the healthy baseline over five telemetry channels, smoothed with an exponentially weighted moving average (α = 0.2) |
| Alarm rule | Smoothed score above the 99.5th percentile of the healthy baseline for three consecutive observations |
| Planning agent | Prioritizes alarmed units by score, subject to a maintenance crew capacity of two simultaneous interventions |
| Version | 0.1, pilot |
| Code | [05_Reproducible_ML_Pipeline](../05_Reproducible_ML_Pipeline/) |

## Intended Use

- **Primary use:** research on condition monitoring and maintenance planning for underground extraction equipment.
- **Mode:** recommendation to a human maintenance planner; the agents never execute interventions.
- **Out of scope:** autonomous control of equipment and safety-critical decisions without human review.

## Factors

Equipment type, duty cycle, degradation heterogeneity across units, connectivity probability and crew capacity.

## Metrics

Fleet availability, unplanned failures, preventive interventions, unnecessary interventions (performed while health is above 0.6) and maintenance cost in relative units.

## Evaluation Data

Synthetic fleet telemetry generated by the simulator (see [Datasheet_v0.1.md](Datasheet_v0.1.md)); 30 paired replications.

## Quantitative Analyses (pilot)

| Policy | Availability | Unplanned failures | Preventive interventions | Maintenance cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reactive | 0.972 | 25.83 | 0.00 | 258.3 |
| Calendar-based (450 h) | 0.990 | 0.73 | 39.30 | 85.9 |
| Digital twin agents | 0.987 | 0.00 | 57.67 | 115.3 |

Means over 30 replications; confidence intervals and paired tests in `results/` of the pipeline.

## Ethical Considerations

Synthetic data contain no personal data. Real telemetry may include operator identifiers, which must be pseudonymized under Law N° 29733.

## Caveats and Recommendations

The agents were evaluated only on synthetic data. In the pilot they eliminate unplanned failures but intervene earlier than necessary, raising maintenance cost above the calendar-based policy; the alarm threshold must be recalibrated before any field use.
